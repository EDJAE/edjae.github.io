About Me

Building things, breaking things, learning either way.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Tangub City Global College (TCGC), working through coursework in software development,research methods, and cybersecurity and enjoying almost all of it more than I expected to.

My objective is simple: become a software engineer who can move comfortably between the front end, the back end, and the database and who understands the security implications of every layer in between. Recent work includes a full suite of PHP + MySQL CRUD APIs, a browser-based lexical analyzer, and a cybersecurity case study on the Philippine Senate website defacement incident.

Outside of class, I'm usually deep in a personal tool, a thesis draft, or a rabbit hole about how compilers actually work.
